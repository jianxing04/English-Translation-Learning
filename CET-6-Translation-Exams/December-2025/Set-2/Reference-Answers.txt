节俭 (frugality) 是中华民族的传统美德。
Frugality is a traditional virtue of the Chinese nation.

在中国古代，生产力低下，先人们深知劳动成果来之不易，因而秉持节俭的理念。
In ancient China, when productivity was extremely low, our ancestors, deeply aware that the fruits of labor were hard-earned, firmly upheld the principle of frugality.

到了物质极大丰富的今天，中国人民依然坚持节俭的生活方式，体现了理性消费的生活理念和对劳动的尊重。
Today, in an era of material abundance, the Chinese people still maintain a frugal lifestyle, which reflects a philosophy of rational consumption and a deep respect for labor.

近年来，中国政府持续加强节约型社会的建设，倡导“光盘行动”，减少食物浪费，提倡简约低碳的生活方式，推进资源循环利用，反对过度消费。
In recent years, the Chinese government has continued to step up efforts to build a conservation-minded society, promoting the "Clean Plate" campaign, reducing food waste, advocating a simple and low-carbon lifestyle, promoting resource recycling, and opposing overconsumption.

节约型社会的建设有力地推动了中国经济的高质量发展。
Efforts to build a conservation-minded society have significantly contributed to the high-quality growth of China's economy.
