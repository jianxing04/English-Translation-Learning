中华民族崇尚自强自立的精神，这一精神深植于中国文化之中，是民族生存与发展的重要支撑。
The Chinese nation holds the spirit of self-reliance and independence in high esteem. Deeply rooted in Chinese culture, this spirit serves as a vital foundation for the nation's survival and development.

改革开放以来，中国在自强自立的征程上，持续加大科研投入，在信息技术、人工智能、航天工程等领域不断取得新突破。
Since reform and opening-up, China has pursued greater self-reliance and independence by continuously increasing investment in scientific research and achieving new breakthroughs in fields such as information technology, artificial intelligence, and aerospace engineering.

5G 网络覆盖全国，远程医疗发展迅速，电子支付的使用全球领先，嫦娥探月工程展现了航天领域引人注目的发展。
5G networks have achieved nationwide coverage, telemedicine has developed rapidly, and China leads the world in the use of electronic payments. The Chang'e lunar exploration program has also demonstrated remarkable progress in the aerospace sector.

自强自主精神成为推动国家现代化进程的强大动力，激励着中国人民为实现民族复兴 (rejuvenation) 的中国梦不断奋进。
The spirit of self-reliance and independence has become a powerful driving force behind China's modernization, inspiring the Chinese people to forge ahead tirelessly toward realizing the Chinese Dream of national rejuvenation.
