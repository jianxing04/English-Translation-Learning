中国的传统婚礼习俗历史悠久，从周朝开始就逐渐形成了一套完整的婚礼仪式，有些一直沿用至今。
Traditional Chinese wedding customs have a long history. A complete set of wedding rituals began to take shape during the Zhou Dynasty, and some of them are still practiced today.

如今的中式婚礼习俗已有很大变化，但婚礼庆典仍然十分隆重。
Contemporary Chinese wedding customs have changed significantly, but wedding celebrations remain grand.

婚礼场地经过精心装饰，以象征喜庆 (jubilance) 的红色为主色调，摆放着许多祝愿新人幸福的物件。
With red, a symbol of jubilance, as the dominant color, the wedding venue is carefully decorated with many items symbolizing wishes for the newlyweds' happiness.

在婚礼上，新人要拜天地 (bow to Heaven and Earth)、拜父母和相互对拜，然后设宴招待宾客，并向宾客敬酒致谢。
At the wedding, the newlyweds bow to Heaven and Earth, to their parents, and to each other. They then host a banquet for the guests and offer toasts to express their gratitude.
