中国盛产竹子，是最早开发利用竹资源的国家。
China is rich in bamboo resources and was the earliest country to develop and utilize them.

竹子在中国分布广泛，品种丰富。
Bamboo is widely distributed in China and comes in a wide variety of species.

竹子实用性强，用于生产和生活的许多方面，如筷子、桌椅的制作和桥梁、房屋的建造。
Bamboo is highly versatile and is widely used in many aspects of production and daily life, such as making chopsticks, tables and chairs, and building bridges and houses.

中国人爱竹，自古以来就有无数文人以竹为主题，创作了绚丽多彩的文学和绘画作品。
Chinese people have long loved bamboo. Since ancient times, countless scholars and artists have created a rich variety of literary works and paintings with bamboo as their theme.

竹子主干 (stem) 笔直，象征正直的品格。
Bamboo has a straight stem, which symbolizes integrity.

竹子具有强大的生命力和适应能力，无论环境多么恶劣，都能够顽强生存，因而寓意坚韧不拔的精神。
Bamboo has strong vitality and adaptability and can survive tenaciously even in extremely harsh environments, thus symbolizing the spirit of perseverance and resilience.

几千年来，竹子一直被视为中华民族品格的象征。
For thousands of years, bamboo has been regarded as a symbol of the character of the Chinese nation.
