扇子自古以来就深受中国人喜爱，但现在已不只是消暑纳凉的工具，而更多地作为艺术品供人欣赏。
Fans, which have long been cherished by Chinese people, are no longer merely tools for keeping cool in summer, but also artworks for people to appreciate.

许多扇子造型优美、做工精良，并绘有山水、花鸟、人物等精美图案，具有很高的艺术价值。
Many fans have elegant designs, are exquisitely crafted, and are decorated with exquisite patterns of landscapes, flowers, birds and figures, giving them great artistic value.

中国许多著名画家和书法家喜欢在扇子上作诗绘画，展示其艺术品味。
Many famous Chinese painters and calligraphers enjoy inscribing poems and creating paintings on fans to showcase their artistic taste.

扇子常作为礼物赠予他人，表达美好的祝福和真挚的情感。
Fans are often given as gifts to others to express good wishes and sincere feelings.

如今，扇子的实用功能已大为减弱，但作为一种文化符号和艺术形式，扇子仍然在中国传统文化中扮演着重要角色。
Nowadays, the practical function of fans has greatly diminished. However, as a cultural symbol and art form, fans still play a vital role in traditional Chinese culture.
