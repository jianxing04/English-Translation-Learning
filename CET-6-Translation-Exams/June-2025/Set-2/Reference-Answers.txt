自古以来，中国的水资源北缺南丰，分布极不均衡。
Since ancient times, China's water resources have been scarce in the north and abundant in the south, with an extremely uneven distribution.

为了有效解决北方严重缺水问题，中国政府实施了南水北调工程 (the South-to-North Water Diversion Project)。
In order to effectively address the severe water shortage in northern China, the Chinese government has implemented the South-to-North Water Diversion Project.

这是一项跨区域配置水资源的宏大水利工程。
This is a large-scale water conservancy project designed to allocate water resources across regions.

历经数十年的规划与筹备，工程于 2002 年开工建设，分为东、中、西 3 条线路，总长 4350 公里，惠及人口将超过 4亿。
After decades of planning and preparation, construction of the project began in 2002. It consists of three routes—the eastern, central and western routes—with a total length of 4,350 kilometers, and will benefit more than 400 million people.

自 2014 年通水以来，工程向北方调水累计已超 500 亿立方米，为北方地区的人民提供了可靠的水资源，同时也极大地改善了这一地区的生态环境，促进了经济的可持续健康发展。
Since it began supplying water in 2014, the project has transferred a total of more than 50 billion cubic meters of water to northern China, providing a reliable water supply for people in the region. At the same time, it has significantly improved the local ecological environment and promoted sustainable and healthy economic development.
